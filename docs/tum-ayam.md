
# Tum Ayam (Minced Chicken in Banana Leaf)

??? note "Ingredients"

    - 600 g boneless chicken, skin removed and minced
    - 1 tbsp fried shallots
    - 1 bowl fried sambal (garlic, shallot, red chilli)
    - 80 ml coconut cream
    - 3 tbsp spice paste for chicken
    - 1 calamansi
    - Pinch of salt, to taste
    - Pinch of crushed black peppercorns, to taste
    - 12 banana leaf pieces, cut into 25 × 18 cm squares
    - 12 salam leaves (optional fresh or dry kaffir lime leaves)

??? note "Method"

    1. Combine the ingredients except banana leaf and mix well.
    2. Place “a halved table spoon” of mixture, as worded in the source, in the centre of a banana leaf on top of a salam leaf and wrap.
    3. Steam parcels for about 10 minutes, until well cooked.

??? note "Notes"

    - Tum are leaf-wrapped bundles of highly seasoned food made in Bali with ingredients ranging from eels to chicken, pork, beef or duck.

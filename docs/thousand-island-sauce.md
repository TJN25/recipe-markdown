
# Thousand Island Sauce

??? note "Ingredients"

    - 150 g mayonnaise
    - 2 tbsp tomato ketchup
    - 1½ tbsp gherkin relish
    - ¼ tsp onion powder
    - ¼ tsp sweet paprika
    - ½ tsp lemon juice or cider vinegar, plus more to taste
    - A little black pepper
    - Salt, only if needed

??? note "Method"

    1. Mix everything together and leave in the fridge for 15–30 minutes, then taste.
    2. If too creamy or bland, add another ½ tbsp gherkin relish. If too sweet, add lemon juice or cider vinegar, ¼ tsp at a time. If too sharp, add a small pinch of sugar.
    3. For a Reuben, taste a little with the meat, cheese and sauerkraut before adding more acidity.


# Dadar Unti (Green Pancakes with Coconut)

??? note "Ingredients"

    ??? info "Batter"

        - 1 cup flour
        - ¼ tsp salt
        - ½ tsp vanilla
        - 1 cup water
        - 1 egg, lightly beaten
        - 1½ tbsp sugar
        - ¼ cup coconut milk
        - 1 tbsp lemon juice (optional)

    ??? info "Filling"

        - 1 cup grated coconut
        - 1 pandan leaf or pandan essence
        - 1 tbsp vanilla essence
        - 4 tbsp palm sugar

??? note "Method"

    ??? info "Batter"

        1. Blend batter ingredients until smooth and lump-free. Set aside for a few minutes while making filling.

    ??? info "Filling"

        1. Mix filling ingredients thoroughly. Cook over low heat for 3 minutes, until coconut is soft.

    ??? info "Assembly"

        1. Heat a frying pan over low heat. Add a smear of oil, then 1–2 tbsp batter, tilting the pan so it reaches the edges. Pancakes should be very thin.
        2. When bubbles appear, flip and cook for a few seconds on the other side.
        3. Add a smear of butter when necessary and repeat until batter is finished.
        4. Fill each pancake with 2–3 tbsp coconut filling and fold like an envelope. Makes 8.

??? note "Notes"

    - To make the pancakes green, add pandan essence to the batter.


# Rendang Sapi (Beef Rendang)

??? note "Ingredients"

    ??? info "Beef"

        - 1 kg beef topside, cut into bite-sized pieces
        - 500 ml thin coconut milk
        - 1000 ml thick coconut milk
        - 1–2 stalks lemongrass, knotted
        - 2 cm cinnamon stick
        - 1 whole star anise
        - 1–2 fresh turmeric leaves (optional)
        - 2–3 kaffir lime leaves
        - 2 salam leaves
        - 1 cup roasted grated coconut
        - 5 cloves
        - Oil (quantity not specified in source)

    ??? info "Spice paste"

        - 200 g shallots, chopped
        - 100 g garlic, chopped
        - 100 g galangal, chopped
        - 1 tsp cumin seeds
        - 80 g ginger, chopped
        - 300 g red chilli, chopped
        - 1 tsp nutmeg
        - 1 tsp coriander seeds

??? note "Method"

    ??? info "Spice paste"

        1. Grind or blend all spice paste ingredients to a fine paste.

    ??? info "Beef"

        1. Heat oil over high heat and stir-fry spice paste until fragrant, 1–2 minutes. Add beef and remaining ingredients and bring to a simmer. The source says “simmer/reduce for 2 hours” here.
        2. The next source step says to reduce to medium heat and simmer uncovered for about 1 hour, stirring constantly, then reduce to low and simmer for another hour, stirring occasionally, until meat is very tender and most liquid has evaporated.
        3. Once oil separates from the milk, stir-fry beef in that oil for about 25 minutes until dark brown. Remove from heat.

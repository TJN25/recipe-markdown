
# Soto Ayam (Chicken Noodle Soup)

??? note "Ingredients"

    ??? info "Spice paste"

        - 30 ml coconut oil
        - 50 g garlic
        - 30 g candlenuts
        - 20 g turmeric
        - 15 g ginger
        - 5 kaffir lime leaves
        - 1 stalk lemongrass, bruised
        - 10 g red chilli
        - 1 tbsp coriander seeds

    ??? info "Soup"

        - 1 whole chicken/chicken breast
        - 3 litres water, for stock
        - 3 kaffir lime leaves
        - 1½ tsp salt
        - ½ tsp ground white pepper
        - 200 g dried glass noodles or rice vermicelli, soaked in water to soften
        - 1 hard-boiled egg, peeled and quartered
        - 50 g bean sprouts, blanched in hot water
        - 50 g white cabbage, shredded
        - 2 sprigs celery
        - 10 g crispy fried shallots
        - 2 slices lime, to serve
        - 2 tbsp sliced Chinese celery, to garnish
        - 1 tomato, cubed
        - 1 portion sambal soto

    ??? info "Sambal soto"

        - 10 g garlic
        - 5 g hot chilli
        - 10 g red chilli
        - 5 ml vinegar (the source method instead gives 2 tsp)
        - 100 ml water, for boiling
        - Pinch of salt

??? note "Method"

    ??? info "Spice paste"

        1. Grind all spice paste ingredients to a smooth paste.

    ??? info "Soup"

        1. Clean and rinse chicken. Bring water to a boil over high heat, immerse chicken and boil for 15 minutes. Turn off heat and leave chicken to steep for 15–20 minutes.
        2. Remove and drain chicken. Strain and reserve stock. Separate meat from bones and shred thinly.
        3. Bring 2 litres reserved stock to a boil with spice paste, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves and celery leaves.
        4. Divide softened noodles and shredded chicken among bowls. Ladle over hot broth and top with egg, bean sprouts and cabbage.

    ??? info "Sambal soto"

        1. Boil sambal ingredients with water “until the water reducing ¾ quarter”, as worded in the source. Blend to a paste, then add a pinch of salt and 2 tsp vinegar.

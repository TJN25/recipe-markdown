
# Baked Beef Koftas

??? note "Ingredients"

    ??? info "Koftas"

        - 8 wooden skewers (no need to soak)
        - 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling
        - 500 g beef mince or lamb mince
        - 2 tsp ground cumin
        - 1 tsp dried chilli flakes (reduce or omit)
        - 1 tsp cooking salt
        - ½ tsp black pepper
        - 1 brown onion, grated
        - ¾ cup (45 g) panko breadcrumbs
        - ¼ cup (10 g) finely minced parsley
        - 1 egg

    ??? info "Vegetables"

        - 2 large tomatoes, cut into 1 cm thick slices
        - 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
        - ½ tsp cooking salt
        - ½ tsp black pepper
        - 1 large red onion, halved and cut into 1 cm wedges
        - 2 zucchini, cut into 7 mm thick slices

    ??? info "Tomato sauce"

        - 2 tsp tomato paste
        - 1 tbsp lemon juice
        - 2 tbsp water
        - ¼ tsp cooking salt
        - ¼ tsp black pepper

    ??? info "To serve"

        - 2 tbsp finely chopped parsley
        - 4 Lebanese breads or other flatbreads, slightly warmed
        - Lemon yoghurt sauce

??? note "Method"

    ??? info "Koftas"

        1. Preheat the oven to 240°C (220°C fan-forced).
        2. Mix the mince, spices, salt, pepper, onion, breadcrumbs, parsley and egg with your hands. Divide equally among 8 skewers, shaping into 2.5 cm wide cylinders. Place in the middle of a large baking tray, close but not touching.

    ??? info "Vegetables"

        1. Place tomato slices on one side of the tray, slightly overlapping. Drizzle with half the oil and sprinkle with half the salt and pepper.
        2. On the other side, toss red onion and zucchini with the remaining oil, salt and pepper. Spread out.

    ??? info "Tomato sauce"

        1. Mix sauce ingredients and pour evenly over the koftas. Drizzle koftas with the 1 tbsp olive oil.

    ??? info "To serve"

        1. Bake for 20 minutes and rest for 3 minutes. Toss vegetables with pan juices and sprinkle with parsley. Serve with warmed breads and lemon yoghurt sauce for making mini kofta kebabs.

??? note "Notes"

    - Beef or lamb both work well.
    - Skewers can be omitted; shape free-form koftas instead.
    - Substitute other quick-cooking vegetables such as broccolini, asparagus, green beans or capsicum.
    - Use flatbreads or pita pockets suitable for stuffing or wrapping, or serve over rice or couscous.
    - Loosen sauce with a little extra water if needed to make it pourable, keeping it thick enough to blanket the koftas. It gains flavour from the beef and vegetable pan juices.
    - Do not waste the tray juices.
    - Leftovers: refrigerate for 3 days; not suitable for freezing.

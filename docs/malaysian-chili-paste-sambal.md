
# Malaysian Chili Paste (Sambal)

??? note "Ingredients"

    - 25 g dried chillies, cut and deseeded
    - ¼ onion
    - 2 cloves garlic
    - ½ slice ginger
    - ½ thumb-sized piece shrimp paste (optional)
    - ½ cup water (125 ml)
    - Oil, for cooking
    - ½ tbsp lemon juice
    - 1½ tbsp coconut sugar
    - ½ tsp salt

??? note "Method"

    1. Soak chillies in hot water for 30 minutes, then drain.
    2. Blend chillies, onion, garlic, ginger, shrimp paste and measured water into a smooth paste using a food processor.
    3. Heat some oil over medium heat. Add the paste and cook for 15–20 minutes, until thickened, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking. Use a splash guard.
    4. Stir in lemon juice, sugar and salt. Cook for another 1–2 minutes.

??? note "Notes"

    - Aim for slightly more sweetness than heat, saltiness or sourness; adjust to taste.
    - Shrimp paste can be omitted or replaced with ½ tbsp fish sauce.
    - Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 6 months.

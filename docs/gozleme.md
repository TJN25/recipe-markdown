
# Gozleme

??? note "Ingredients"

    ??? info "Pastry"

        - 375g plain flour
        - 85ml extra virgin olive oil
        - 185ml water
        - 1/2 tsp salt

    ??? info "Spinach and feta filling"

        - 300g baby spinach
        - 2 cloves garlic, minced
        - 2 eggs
        - 1 tsp black pepper
        - 300g Danish feta
        - 200g shredded mozzarella

    ??? info "Spiced beef or lamb filling"

        - 1 tbsp olive oil
        - 2 cloves garlic, minced
        - 1 onion, finely chopped
        - 500g beef or lamb mince
        - 1 capsicum, chopped
        - 90g baby spinach
        - 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
        - 1 tsp paprika
        - 1/2 tsp cayenne, to taste
        - 3/4 tsp salt
        - 3/4 tsp black pepper
        - 1 tbsp water
        - 120g Danish feta

    ??? info "Cooking and serving"

        - 1-1 1/2 tbsp olive oil per gozleme
        - Lemon wedges, optional

??? note "Method"

    ??? info "Pastry"

        1. Combine flour, olive oil, water, and salt in a stand mixer fitted with the dough hook. Mix on medium-high for 2 1/2 minutes, until the dough is smooth, pliable, and not sticky.
        2. Cover and rest for 20 minutes.
        3. Flour the work surface, cut the dough into 4 pieces, and roll each piece into a 35cm x 20cm rectangle.

    ??? info "Spiced beef or lamb filling"

        1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over high heat.
        2. Cook garlic and onion for 1 minute. Add capsicum and cook for 2 minutes.
        3. Add mince and cook, breaking it up, until it changes from pink to brown.
        4. Add cumin, paprika, cayenne, salt, pepper, and water. Cook for 2 minutes.
        5. Add spinach and stir until wilted.
        6. Cool before using.

    ??? info "Spinach and feta filling"

        1. Put spinach, garlic, eggs, and pepper in a bowl.
        2. Scrunch with your hands to reduce the volume and make the spinach floppy.
        3. Add feta and stir through.

    ??? info "Assembly"

        1. Choose one filling, or make a half batch of each.
        2. Spread filling over half of each pastry rectangle, pushing it to the edge.
        3. For the beef or lamb filling, sprinkle crumbled feta over the filling. For the spinach and feta filling, sprinkle mozzarella over the filling.
        4. Fold the pastry over the filling. Press the edges to seal, pressing out air pockets as you go. Use water to help seal if needed.
        5. Flour a pizza paddle or similar flat board and slide the gozleme onto it.

    ??? info "Cooking"

        1. Heat 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
        2. Slide the gozleme into the skillet. Cook for about 3 minutes, pressing lightly, until deep golden and crispy.
        3. Flip and cook the second side, pressing lightly, until crispy.
        4. Transfer to a board, cut into 6 or 8 pieces, and serve immediately with lemon wedges if using.

??? note "Notes"

    - Choose either the spinach and feta filling or the spiced beef or lamb filling. Each filling quantity is enough for 4 gozleme.
    - Danish feta is a soft feta, but normal feta also works.
    - Mozzarella is optional and not traditional.
    - The dough should be soft and pliable but not sticky. Adjust with a little extra flour or water if needed. Hand kneading takes about 6-7 minutes.
    - Gluten-free flour works, but the dough is less stretchy and behaves more like short pastry. Smaller gozleme are recommended.
    - Cooked gozleme are best eaten hot and crispy.
    - Dough can be kept in the fridge for 3-4 days.
    - Do not refrigerate filled uncooked gozleme, because the filling makes the underside of the dough soggy.
    - To keep cooked gozleme warm while cooking the rest, use a 120C oven on a rack.
    - Reheat cooked gozleme by pan frying or baking on a tray at 180C for about 7 minutes.


# Lawar Bali (Balinese Salad)

??? note "Ingredients"

    ??? info "Salad"

        - 600 g young jackfruit, snake beans or green papaya, blanched and cut
        - 1 cup grated coconut, roasted
        - 2 tbsp fried sambal (shallot, garlic, red chilli)
        - 1 tbsp fried shallots
        - 1 tbsp spice paste for chicken

    ??? info "Dressing I"

        - 150 g boneless chicken, minced
        - 2 tbsp spice paste for chicken
        - 1 banana leaf, 30 cm square
        - Pinch of salt
        - Pinch of black pepper

    ??? info "Dressing II"

        - 150 g minced chicken
        - 2 tbsp spice paste for chicken
        - 125 ml chicken stock
        - 100 ml coconut cream
        - Pinch of salt
        - Pinch of black pepper
        - 1 tsp lime juice
        - 1 tbsp cooking oil

??? note "Method"

    ??? info "Dressing I"

        1. Mix chicken with spice paste. Place lengthwise in the centre of the banana leaf and roll tightly.
        2. Grill for 20–25 minutes. Remove leaf and break meat up with a fork to its original minced form.

    ??? info "Dressing II"

        1. Heat oil, add spice paste and sauté until fragrant. Add chicken and sauté until it changes colour.
        2. Stir in stock, bring to a boil and simmer for 2–3 minutes. Add coconut cream and simmer for 1 minute.
        3. Season with salt, black pepper and lime juice. Cool to room temperature.

    ??? info "Salad"

        1. Combine vegetables, spice paste, grated coconut, fried sambal, fried shallots and both chicken dressings. Mix well.
        2. Season to taste with salt, black pepper and lime juice. Garnish with crispy fried shallots.

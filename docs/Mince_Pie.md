# Recipe

##Ingredients
    ??? info "Filling"
        - Mince (500g)
        - Onion (1)
        - Garlic (1 tsp crushed)
        - Beef [stock](stock.md) (1 cube with 250 mL water)
        - Tomato Paste ( 1 Tbsp)
        - Worchesterchire sauce (1 Tbsp)
        - Vegemite (1 tsp)
        - Italian herbs (1 tsp)
        - Cornflour (2 tsp in 1/4 cup of water)
    ??? info "Pie"
        - Puff pastry (3 sheets)
        - Cheese (enough slices to cover the top of the filling)

??? note "Instructions"
    1. Prepare all ingredients for filling (execpt Cornflour as standard and cook in pan.
    2. Mix in the cornflour near the end.
    3. Cook until the desired consistency.
    4. Add Puff pastry to an oven dish lined with baking paper.
    5. Add filling to the dish.
    6. Add Cheese to the top of the filling.
    7. Cover the top with more Puff pastry
    8. Bake at 185C for 40-50 minutes.


# Quick Tartare-Style Sauce

??? note "Ingredients"

    - 150 g mayonnaise
    - 2 tbsp gherkin relish
    - 1 tsp American yellow mustard
    - 1–2 tsp lemon juice
    - ¼ tsp onion powder
    - ⅛ tsp garlic powder
    - Freshly ground black pepper
    - A tiny pinch of salt, if needed

??? note "Method"

    1. Mix everything together, starting with 1 tsp lemon juice. Taste and adjust.
    2. Leave for 10–15 minutes so the dried onion and garlic can hydrate and mellow.

??? note "Notes"

    - Makes roughly 180–200 ml.
    - If the relish is sweet, use the full 2 tsp lemon juice to keep the sauce sharp enough for fish.


# Nasi Goreng - Nia Cooking Class (Fried Rice with Chicken)

??? note "Ingredients"

    - 2 tbsp coconut oil
    - 2 cloves garlic
    - 1 shallot
    - 1 carrot
    - 2 large red chillies
    - 2 bird’s eye chillies
    - 100 g white cabbage
    - 1 tbsp spiced tomato sauce
    - 200 g diced chicken
    - 2 tbsp light soy sauce
    - 2 tbsp sweet soy sauce
    - 2 tbsp oyster sauce
    - Fish sauce, slightly less than 1 tbsp
    - 2 eggs
    - 600 g cooked rice
    - 25 g celery
    - 50 g leek
    - 50 g spinach
    - Pinch of salt
    - 2 tbsp fried shallots
    - ¼ tsp MSG
    - Salt for chicken, 1% of chicken weight
    - A little salt-reduced chicken stock

??? note "Method"

    1. Salt chicken at 1% of its weight and leave for 20 minutes before cooking.
    2. Heat oil in a wok or heavy frying pan. Add shallots, garlic, carrot and chillies. Sauté for 1 minute until shallots and garlic are golden brown.
    3. Add cabbage, spiced tomato sauce and chicken. Sauté for another minute. Season with soy sauce and sauté until dry.
    4. Add eggs and scramble, then add rice. Cook for 3 minutes, then add remaining ingredients except fried shallots.
    5. Mix well, season with salt as necessary and garnish with fried shallots.
    6. Add the fish sauce, oyster sauce, MSG and a little salt-reduced chicken stock during frying; taste and adjust seasoning.

??? note "Notes"

    - Personal cooking adjustments: a half batch of Mie Goreng used ½ shallot and 1 garlic clove and worked well. The full recipe serves 2 and uses 1 shallot and 2 cloves garlic. Reduce fish sauce slightly. Used ¼ tsp MSG in the half batch and felt it could have been slightly more seasoned. Salted chicken at 1% by weight 20 minutes before cooking and added a little salt-reduced chicken stock. Apply these adjustments to Nasi Goreng too.

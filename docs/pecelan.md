
# Pecelan (Vegetable Salad in Peanut Dressing)

??? note "Ingredients"

    ??? info "Salad"

        - 100 g long beans, cut and blanched
        - 100 g bean sprouts, blanched
        - 100 g spinach, blanched
        - 100 g cabbage, thinly sliced and blanched
        - 2 tbsp fried shallots
        - 2 tbsp fried peanuts, crushed
        - Pinch of salt

    ??? info "Peanut sauce"

        - 250 g skin-on peanuts, deep-fried until lightly brown
        - 3 cloves garlic, peeled and sliced
        - 1–3 bird’s eye chillies, finely sliced
        - 10 g kencur, finely sliced
        - 2 tbsp sweet soy sauce (kecap manis)
        - 20 g palm sugar, chopped
        - 1 cup water
        - Pinch of salt, to taste

??? note "Method"

    ??? info "Peanut sauce"

        1. Grind all sauce ingredients except water very finely.
        2. Gradually add water to the desired consistency and season to taste.

    ??? info "Salad"

        1. Combine vegetables with peanut sauce and mix well. Season with salt and pepper.
        2. Garnish with fried shallots and peanuts. Serve with deep-fried crackers.


# Sate Lilit Ayam (Minced Chicken Satay)

??? note "Ingredients"

    - 500 g minced chicken
    - 4–5 tbsp spice paste for chicken
    - 5 lime leaves, chopped
    - 3 tbsp fried shallots
    - 1 tbsp palm sugar syrup
    - Pinch of salt, to taste
    - 1 cup sambal goreng
    - ½ cup coconut milk
    - Lemongrass or satay skewers
    - 1 cup grated coconut

??? note "Method"

    1. Mix grated coconut, chicken spice paste, palm sugar syrup, fried shallots, salt and lime leaves by hand until thoroughly blended.
    2. Mix in chicken and knead together. The mixture should be fragrant, deep yellow and slightly sticky. The source says: “If it is not too dry, you can add water or a little coconut milk.”
    3. Take a dessertspoon of mixture and shape into a pear. Place on the end of a stick and spiral firmly around it until halfway up, resembling a drumstick.
    4. Grill until golden brown.

??? note "Notes"

    - Serve as a main course with rice, or as part of a buffet or cocktails.

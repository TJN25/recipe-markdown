# Recipe

##Ingredients
- Mince (500g)
- Egg (1)
- Onion (1/2)
- Garlic (1 tsp crushed)
- Worchesterchire sauce (A splash)
- Bread crumbs (1 cup)
- Backyard BBQ Seasoning (2 heaped tsp)

??? note "Instructions"
    1. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl.
    2. Cook in the oven for 25 minutes at 185C.

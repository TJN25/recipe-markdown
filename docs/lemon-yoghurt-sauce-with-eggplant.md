
# Lemon Yoghurt Sauce (with Eggplant)

??? note "Ingredients"

    ??? info "Lemon yoghurt sauce"

        - 1 cup (260 g) Greek yoghurt or other plain yoghurt
        - 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
        - 1 tbsp lemon juice
        - 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
        - ¼ tsp cooking salt
        - ¼ tsp black pepper

    ??? info "Roasted eggplant"

        - 2 large eggplants (800 g total), cut into 3 cm cubes
        - 3 tbsp olive oil
        - ¾ tsp cooking salt
        - ¼ tsp black pepper

    ??? info "To serve"

        - 2 tsp extra-virgin olive oil
        - 2 tbsp toasted pine nuts or roughly chopped roasted almonds (or other nuts of choice)
        - 1 tsp chopped coriander leaves
        - Pinch of sumac or paprika

??? note "Method"

    ??? info "Lemon yoghurt sauce"

        1. Mix all sauce ingredients together. Set aside for 10 minutes to let the flavours develop.

    ??? info "Roasted eggplant"

        1. Preheat the oven to 240°C (220°C fan-forced). Line a large baking tray with baking paper.
        2. Toss eggplant with oil, then salt and pepper. Spread on the tray and roast for 30 minutes, tossing at the 20-minute mark. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes.

    ??? info "To serve"

        1. Smear ½ cup (125 g) sauce on a plate and pile eggplant on top. Dollop another ¼ cup (60 g) sauce over it. Drizzle with extra-virgin olive oil and sprinkle with pine nuts, coriander and sumac.

??? note "Notes"

    - The author leaves the eggplant skin on: bitterness has largely been bred out and the skin helps pieces hold together during roasting. Remove it if preferred.
    - The sauce works with asparagus, green beans, broccoli, broccolini, carrots, cauliflower, pumpkin, sweet potato and zucchini.
    - Sauce leftovers: refrigerate for 3 days; not suitable for freezing.
    - Assembled eggplant: best eaten immediately, though it keeps refrigerated for 2 days.

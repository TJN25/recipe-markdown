
# Base Be Siap (Spice Paste for Chicken)

??? note "Ingredients"

    - 350 g shallots, peeled and sliced
    - 190 g garlic, peeled and sliced
    - 75 g kencur (lesser galangal), washed and sliced
    - 90 g galangal (laos), peeled and sliced
    - 25 g candlenuts, crushed (raw peanuts without skin)
    - 190 g turmeric, peeled and sliced
    - 30 g bird’s eye chillies, finely sliced
    - 30 g palm sugar, chopped
    - 100 ml coconut or vegetable oil
    - 3 stalks lemongrass, bruised
    - 4 salam leaves (optional dry or fresh kaffir lime leaves)
    - 375 ml water
    - 1 tbsp salt

??? note "Method"

    1. Combine all ingredients except lime leaves and lemongrass.
    2. Place the ground ingredients in a pressure cooker, add remaining ingredients and bring to a simmer.
    3. Pressure cook at a gauge pressure of 1 bar / 15 psi for 30 minutes, starting when full pressure is reached.
    4. Let the cooker cool for 20 minutes, then cool in the refrigerator overnight.
    5. Spread into an ice cube tray and freeze. Store in an airtight container or freezer bag.

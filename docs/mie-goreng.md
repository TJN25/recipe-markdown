
# Mie Goreng (Fried Noodles)

??? note "Ingredients"

    - 3 tbsp vegetable oil
    - 100 g chicken thigh, deboned and diced into 1 cm cubes
    - 20 g large red chillies, halved, seeded and sliced
    - 50 g cabbage, sliced
    - 2 cloves garlic, sliced
    - 1 shallot, sliced
    - 3 tbsp spiced tomato sauce (sambal tomat)
    - 2 tbsp light soy sauce
    - 2 eggs, beaten
    - Fish sauce, slightly less than 2 tbsp
    - 1 tbsp oyster sauce
    - 2 tbsp sweet soy sauce
    - 500 g egg noodles, cooked and cooled
    - 30 g caisin, sliced
    - 30 g leek or spring onion, sliced
    - 20 g celery leaves, sliced
    - 2 tbsp fried shallots
    - ¼ tsp MSG
    - Salt for chicken, 1% of chicken weight
    - A little salt-reduced chicken stock

??? note "Method"

    1. Salt chicken at 1% of its weight and leave for 20 minutes before cooking.
    2. Heat oil in a wok or heavy saucepan. Add garlic and shallots, then chicken. Stir-fry for 1 minute until ingredients change colour.
    3. Add chillies and cabbage and sauté for another minute.
    4. Add eggs and cook over high heat until scrambled and cooked.
    5. Add noodles, light soy sauce, sweet soy sauce and tomato sauce. Mix well.
    6. Add caisin, leek and celery and fry for another minute.
    7. Add the fish sauce, oyster sauce, MSG and a little salt-reduced chicken stock during frying; taste and adjust seasoning.

??? note "Notes"

    - Personal cooking adjustments: a half batch of Mie Goreng used ½ shallot and 1 garlic clove and worked well. The full recipe serves 2 and uses 1 shallot and 2 cloves garlic. Reduce fish sauce slightly. Used ¼ tsp MSG in the half batch and felt it could have been slightly more seasoned. Salted chicken at 1% by weight 20 minutes before cooking and added a little salt-reduced chicken stock. Apply these adjustments to Nasi Goreng too.
